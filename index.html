How to structure your data (REMBI / BioImage Archive)
  1. Study: the overall project (ideally the same as your folder name)
  2. Biosample: the organism or source material
  3. Specimen: what was imaged and how it was prepared
  4. Image acquisition: microscope, lens, channels and replicates
  5. Image data: where the raw data are stored
  6. Analysed data: one analysis form per analysis. The same images can be analysed many times.
